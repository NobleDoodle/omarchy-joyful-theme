# Joyful wallpapers from Wikimedia Commons

Cropped to 16:9 and sized to 3840x2160. Marked ⇡ = cropped at full size, upscaled 2x with Omascayl's backend (upscayl-bin, remacri-4x), then downscaled. Marked ◐ = the copper and bronze baubles recolored toward the theme's maroon and the whole image warmed slightly, in PhotoCraft (masked Hue/Saturation + Selective Color, Warming Filter 85 at 20%). Postcards (07–10) fill the screen: 07 is cropped slightly (nothing but margin lost); 08, 09 and 10 are widened to 16:9 by extending their own paper, with texture taken from a plain area of the same card.
